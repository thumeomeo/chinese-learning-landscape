Executive Overview

Chinese is the fastest-growing language in 12 major learning markets while remaining under-monetized through classic affiliate channels. Payouts split across several structures - percentage commission through networks, creator referral codes, negotiated viewer discounts, and flat per-video contracts - and the same creator often runs more than one at a time.

二

Why Chinese, Why Now

Demand-side figures, gathered 10 Aug 2026. Market-size reports vary widely with scope definitions, so two anchor sources are cited rather than an average.

Learner base

Over 200 million people study or use Chinese internationally; the active learner pool sits around 100 million (Ministry of Education data via CGTN and Global Times). 86 countries have placed Chinese inside national education systems as of September 2025, and more than 20,000 private institutions teach it, growing about 10% a year.

Market value

HolonIQ valued Chinese language learning at $7.4 billion in 2023, projected to double by 2027, with more than 6 million paying learners. The online-platform slice alone runs $1.5 billion (2024) toward a projected $3.2 billion by 2033 (Verified Market Reports, about 9.9% CAGR).

Momentum signal

In the 2025 Duolingo Language Report, Chinese ranks eighth by volume but is the fastest-growing language in 12 major markets - Brazil, Indonesia, Thailand, South Korea, France, Germany and others - and second by growth in the United States. The growth story, not the volume story, is the headline.

The core pain point

Unlike European languages, Chinese confronts learners with characters and tones. That single obstacle powers the entire creator niche - mnemonic explainers, tone drills, character breakdowns - and the whole writing-practice tool category. It is the lens used to select the creators and apps below.

How Creators Actually Get Paid

Short-form apps rarely rely on classic cookie-tracked links, but that does not mean they skip affiliate economics. Four structures run in parallel, and a single creator commonly stacks two or three of them at once. Figures from recruiting pages and job listings are performance-marketing claims rather than signed contracts, so treat the upper ranges as ceilings.

码

1 - Referral codes field-confirmed

The creator reads out a personal code; the viewer types it and receives a bonus trial or discount, which simultaneously attributes the sale. Novli is the clearest documented example at 30% for a full year, and the mechanic survives platforms where links never get clicked.

链

2 - Tracked bio links field-confirmed

A deep link in the profile bio carries attribution parameters. Observed live on @chinese.w.chen, whose Instagram bio points at airlearn.onelink.me/FBTy/rtgzvlka - a per-creator tracked install link, which is affiliate attribution regardless of how the payout is structured.

折

3 - Negotiated viewer discounts

Instead of a network, the creator negotiates a discount code directly with the brand and takes a cut or a flat fee per redemption. This is how most products in the "no public program" list below still end up promoted - The Chairman's Bao, Du Chinese and Hack Chinese all issue private partner codes with no public program page.

聘

4 - Flat per-video and retainer deals

PingoAI advertises "$20-$1,000+ per video" with top creators at "$10,000+ per month", requiring daily posting across three platforms. Airlearn hires like a casting call - $500-1,000 monthly base plus $100-1,000 bonuses for videos passing 250K to 2.5M organic views, at 60-150 videos a month. HelloTalk's Global Ambassador Program pays per approved post with stackable view bonuses, advertising up to $10,000 a month, and asks only one post a month at 3-8 hours a week. recruiting-page figures

Field evidence beats directory listings. Two accounts in the watchlist disclose their program in the bio itself - @rogerlearnschinese as a Bunpo Chinese Ambassador and @chinese.w.chen through a tracked Airlearn link. Reading creator bios turned out to be a faster way to confirm a program is live and paying than searching affiliate directories, which are frequently stale.

Strategic Insights

壹

Four payment structures, often stacked

Established course platforms pay percentage commission through networks and expect blog or search traffic. Mobile-first newcomers lean on referral codes, tracked bio links, negotiated viewer discounts and flat per-video contracts. These are not mutually exclusive: a creator can carry a tracked install link, read out a discount code and still hold a monthly retainer. Any pitch or evaluation should name which structures a target app supports rather than asking whether it "has an affiliate program".

貳

Recurring beats headline rates

A 60% one-time payout is worth less than 15% lifetime or 40% across 24 months for a subscriber who stays. Skritter, Bunpo, Mondly's renewal-inclusive 30% and Novli's full-year 30% are the only recurring structures found in the whole survey. For evergreen content that keeps surfacing months after posting, recurring programs compound while one-time payouts do not.

參

Code-based attribution is built for short-form

Novli's mechanic - the viewer types a creator code and gets a 30-day trial - survives an environment where cookies never fire and links go unclicked. It also gives the viewer something concrete, which raises redemption over a bare link. Expect more apps to copy this; it is the cleanest attribution mechanic for exactly the channel this research targets.

肆

Creator bios are the fastest verification source

Affiliate directories go stale - Memrise's program has been dead since at least 2024 while directories still list 15%, and ChinesePod's page now 404s. Meanwhile live creator bios confirmed two active programs in seconds: a declared Bunpo ambassadorship and a per-creator Airlearn tracking link. When auditing whether a program actually pays, check who is promoting it right now before trusting any listing.

八

Method & Limits

法

How figures were sourced

Official program pages and network marketplace listings came first; third-party blog figures appear only when tagged unverified. Every check carries the audit date of 10 August 2026. Traffic, MAU and revenue claims without a direct source were excluded entirely. Creator follower and like counts were read from the live public profiles.

限

Known limits

"Not found" is not proof of absence - private creator deals are invisible by design. Some sites block automated access, including Preply, WuKong and archive services, so a few attribution windows rest on secondary sources. Gated rates at Preply and Busuu only become visible after network approval. Per-video view counts are collected from public profile grids, where pinned posts are excluded because they are not recent.
