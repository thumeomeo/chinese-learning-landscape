# The Chinese Learning Landscape

Market research report: learner demand, creator accounts with consistent reach, and a verified map of
which Chinese-learning apps pay commission and exactly how much.

**Live page:** https://thumeomeo.github.io/chinese-learning-landscape/

## Contents

- Executive overview
- Why Chinese, why now (market sizing with sources)
- Creator watchlist (5 TikTok + 5 Instagram accounts)
- Affiliate programs, verified (Tier 1 published rates, Tier 2 gated rates)
- How creators actually get paid (4 payout structures)
- Apps with no affiliate program
- Strategic insights
- Method and limits

Audit date: 10 August 2026. Every commission figure is tagged by source confidence -
`official` (confirmed on the program's own page or its network listing), `unverified`
(third-party blogs only), or `on request` (program exists, rate private).
